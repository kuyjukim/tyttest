## Description (4000)

Set a timer and a tree grows while it runs. Finish the session and the tree stays in your garden for good. Walk away and it withers.

■ Only what you finished counts

An abandoned session earns nothing. Not species unlocks, not your streak, not your lifetime total - all of them count completed sessions only. An app that gives you half credit for half a session eventually teaches you to finish nothing.

■ Strict mode

With it on, leaving the app during a session kills the tree. With it off, the timer keeps running while you go elsewhere. Which one is right depends on what you are doing that day, so both are here.

■ Seven species

Sprout, pine, maple, ginkgo, willow, cherry, baobab, unlocked by accumulated focus. The trees are computed as they are drawn rather than loaded from a set of pictures, so two trees of the same species are never quite the same tree.

■ The garden

Everything you planted stays, including what withered. Not clearing those away is the honest thing to do, and looking at them is what makes you finish the next one.

■ What you focused on

Write a line at the start of a session and the stats screen groups your time by it. Today, streak and lifetime; seven- and thirty-day charts; your best day.

■ No subscription

Pay once. Using an app that takes money every month to build the habit of concentrating never made much sense.

■ No networking code at all

Not "we don't collect data" - there is no network code in the app. No account, no sign-in, no ads, no leaderboard, no friends list. Your records are kept on this device and the app sends them nowhere. If you have your phone's own backup turned on, this app's file is included in it like every other app's - that is between you and Apple, and the developer cannot see it.

■ Also

· Dark mode, following the system theme
· English and Korean
· A daily goal and a streak
· Haptic feedback, which you can turn off
· Works identically in aeroplane mode

■ What it does not do

No focusing together with friends, no leaderboards, no planting-a-real-tree partnership. Those are all a different kind of app, one that needs a server and an account. This is a timer you use by yourself, and that is why it asks you for nothing.
