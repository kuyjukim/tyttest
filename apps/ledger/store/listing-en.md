## Description (4000)

There are plenty of apps that record what you already spent. Ledger decides before you spend.

Give each envelope an amount at the start of the month - groceries, transport, going out - and what is left stops being a guess. It is not an app that answers "how much do I have left this month?" so much as one that stops you having to ask.

■ How much you can spend today

What is left, divided by the days that are left. Rounded down rather than up, so the number on screen is one you can actually spend every remaining day without running out.

■ Move money between envelopes

Short on groceries? Take it from somewhere else. That edits this month's plan, not your history - the record of what you actually bought stays exactly as it was.

■ Envelopes that carry over

For the things you save up for, what is left rolls into next month. So does an overspend. An envelope that forgives going over at midnight on the 31st is not a budget, it is a tally.

■ Money arithmetic that is actually correct

Decimal places follow the currency - none for won and yen, two for dollars, three for dinars - and every calculation is whole-number. Split an amount three ways and the parts still add back up to the total, to the last cent.

■ No subscription

Pay once. Using an app that takes money every month to manage the money that leaves every month never made much sense.

■ No networking code at all

Not "we don't collect data" - there is no network code in the app. No account, no sign-in, no ads, no bank connection. Your records are on this device, and nothing here can stop working because a server did.

■ Also

· Dark mode
· English and Korean
· Move freely between months; past and future months are both editable
· Spending by envelope, and a daily spending chart
· Archive an envelope to keep its history, or delete it outright
· Won, dollar, yen, euro, pound and dinar

■ What it does not do

There is no bank connection. That needs a server, a regulator and a subscription - a different kind of app. This one asks you to type, and in exchange the numbers are exact and permanently yours.
